About Princess of Olives

Trained in high-quality Rebirthing. Princess of Olives is a space dedicated to human development, personal transformation, and the expansion of consciousness.

Through Rebirthing sessions, I accompany individuals who wish to understand themselves more deeply, release what no longer serves them, and open up to new possibilities for growth.

I believe every human being possesses the capacity to renew themselves when they find the right space to listen, understand, and remember their own inner strength.

Rebirth

Rebirth is an invitation to return to your essence. It doesn't aim to turn you into someone new; it invites you to remember.

Remember your clarity. Remember your strength. Remember your capacity for transformation through conscious breathing.

Each session is unique. Everyone arrives with a different story, needs, and process within a safe and held space.

Who is this path for?

✨ Individuals going through major life changes.
✨ Those seeking greater mental clarity.
✨ People wanting to transform repetitive patterns.
✨ Individuals interested in personal growth.
✨ Anyone feeling the call to know themselves more deeply.
